08 / 09
Historic Markings & Inscriptions

Small Details.
Big History.

H.W. / 87 — Harley Warrick Painter's Mark

The surviving H.W. / 87 marking is identified in this field record as the painter's mark of Harley Warrick, the Ohio Mail Pouch barn painter; “87” records the 1987 marking.

2026 preservation treatment: Denny Smith restored the surviving marking with solid oil-based paint and deliberate single-brush strokes reproducing the surviving hand-painted character.

Historic Incised Carving

Hand-incised markings recorded in place. Visible evidence includes apparent initials and additional characters or symbols. Date, authorship and original significance remain unknown.
