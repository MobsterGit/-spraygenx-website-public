Big History.

❦
HISTORIC MARKINGS / INSCRIPTIONS • DOCUMENTED EXISTING CONDITION

H.W. / 87 — Possible Harley Warrick Painter's Mark

The existing initials and date-like marking were documented prior to treatment. “H.W.” is consistent with the initials of Ohio Mail Pouch painter Harley Warrick, and “87” may indicate 1987. Attribution remains subject to historical verification.

2026 preservation treatment: Denny Smith, Spray GenX LLC, restored the surviving marking with solid oil-based paint. Before restoration, Smith practiced deliberate single-brush strokes to reproduce the character of the surviving hand-painted marks. The treatment followed visible evidence rather than redesigning or modernizing the lettering.

Historic Incised Carving

Hand-incised markings recorded in place. Visible evidence includes apparent initials and additional characters or symbols. Date, authorship and original significance remain unknown.
