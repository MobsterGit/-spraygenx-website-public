Residential Project • Windham, Ohio

Whole-Home Interior Transformation

An older interior brought forward through extensive preparation, repairs, cabinet refinishing, wall and trim coatings, and detailed finish work across multiple rooms.

Interior RenovationCabinet RefinishingSurface RepairResidential Painting
The Project

More than a repaint.

The Windham project required substantial corrective work before the finish coats could ever become the focus. Existing surfaces ranged from worn painted walls and trim to dark wood paneling, damaged bathroom areas, aging cabinetry, and numerous patched or altered surfaces.

The objective was to create a cleaner, brighter and more cohesive interior while retaining and refinishing usable existing components wherever practical.

Spray GenX work highlighted here
  • Wall, ceiling and trim preparation
  • Drywall and surface repairs
  • Kitchen cabinet preparation and refinishing
  • Basement paneling conversion and coating
  • Interior doors, trim and detail painting
  • Bathroom and miscellaneous repair preparation
01 • Kitchen

Cabinet refinishing changed the entire room.

The existing cabinet system was retained and extensively prepared. Doors and drawers were removed, cabinet boxes were cleaned, sanded and prepared, and the complete system was refinished to create a substantially brighter kitchen.

Before / preparation and completed-condition photography.

02 • Basement

Dark paneling to a bright finished interior.

The basement illustrates the amount of preparation hidden underneath a finished paint job. Existing dark panel walls were cleaned, coated and extensively corrected before the finish system was completed.

03 • Repairs & Detail Work

The transformation started underneath the paint.

Damaged and altered areas throughout the house required patching, filling, reconstruction and careful preparation. Bathroom openings, wall repairs, trim transitions and other details were addressed as part of producing a uniform finished interior.

04 • Finished Home

One consistent finish carried through the house.

With the preparation complete, the final coatings tie together the kitchen, hallways, living areas, basement, bedrooms, bathrooms and trim into a much cleaner residential interior.
