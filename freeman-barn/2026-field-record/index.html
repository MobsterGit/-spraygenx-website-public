Historic Markings & Preservation Treatment

Small Details.
Big History.

❦
HISTORIC MARKINGS / INSCRIPTIONS • DOCUMENTED PRIOR TO TREATMENT

H.W. / 87 — Possible Harley Warrick Painter's Mark

The existing initials and date-like marking were documented prior to treatment. “H.W.” is consistent with the initials of noted Ohio Mail Pouch barn painter Harley Warrick, and “87” may represent 1987. The attribution is historically plausible but has not been independently authenticated.

Historic Incised Carving

Hand-incised markings recorded in place. Visible evidence includes apparent initials and additional characters or symbols. Date, authorship and original significance remain unknown.

2026 Preservation Treatment: Denny Smith, Spray GenX LLC, restored the surviving H.W. / 87 marking using solid oil-based paint. Before applying the restoration, Smith practiced deliberate single-brush strokes to reproduce the character of the surviving hand-painted marks. The treatment followed the visible historic evidence rather than redesigning, mechanically tracing, or modernizing the lettering.
