2026 Roof Work

Inspect and replace suspect fasteners with EPDM-equipped screws; prepare loose and failing existing coating; address active water-management details; apply two coats of Henry's fibered silver roof coating.

Condition Language

Observed deterioration includes extensive checking, peeling and delamination of the existing silver coating with areas of exposed, oxidized corrugated metal. No perforation is claimed without direct confirmation.
