About the publication

Reporting built for people who work in the field.

The Commercial Painting & Coatings Report provides accurate, useful and accessible coverage of commercial painting, industrial coatings, estimating, facility maintenance, materials, equipment, safety, regulation and construction activity.

Publication principle

Spray GenX's role as publisher is disclosed. Reporting, source selection and conclusions are kept separate from promotion of the company's services.

Editorial policyCommercial Painting & Coatings Report

Mission and standards

The report is intended as a dependable reference for contractors, estimators, facility managers, manufacturers, distributors, architects, engineers and property owners.

Source quality

Reports prioritize primary documents, government agencies, manufacturers, trade organizations, project owners and established regional or industry publications. Sources used in each report are linked for verification and further reading.

Facts and interpretation

Factual reporting is distinguished from market interpretation. When evidence is incomplete or conditions are changing, the report describes the uncertainty rather than presenting speculation as fact.

Corrections

Material errors are corrected promptly. Significant corrections include a visible note explaining what changed and when. Older reports remain archived so industry developments can be reviewed over time.

Publisher and company references

Spray GenX LLC is identified as the publisher. References to products, companies or Spray GenX services are included only when relevant to the subject and are not presented as independent endorsements.

Reader submissions

Contractors, manufacturers, representatives, facility teams and industry organizations may suggest topics or provide documentation. Submission does not guarantee publication, and material may be independently verified before use.

Scope

Coverage includes local, regional and national developments when they affect commercial painting, protective coatings, construction activity, facility maintenance, safety, materials, labor, equipment or technical practice.

Publisher disclosure

The Commercial Painting & Coatings Report is published by Spray GenX LLC, a commercial painting and refinishing contractor. The publication provides industry reporting beyond the promotion of the company's services.
