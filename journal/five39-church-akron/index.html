Project background

Spray GenX completed interior painting work for Five39 Church in Akron. The project became another important institutional reference within the company's Northeast Ohio commercial portfolio.

Working in an institutional space

From the field
On commercial interiors, the finished coating is only one measure of success. The customer also remembers communication, cleanliness and whether the building remained manageable while the work was underway.

Execution priorities

The focus was dependable preparation, controlled production and a consistent finished appearance. Areas were handled systematically so the project could progress without creating unnecessary disruption.

Why this record matters
