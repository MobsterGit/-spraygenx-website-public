About Spray GenX

Built on Standards. Proven in the Field.

Spray GenX LLC brings decades of commercial, industrial, institutional, and residential coating experience to projects throughout Northeast Ohio.

What defines the work

Specification-driven painting and coatings.

Spray GenX is built around practical field experience, proper preparation, efficient application, and durable finish work. The objective is not simply to make a project look good on completion day — it is to deliver a coating system designed to perform.

Our Standard

Follow the specification. Respect the substrate. Deliver workmanship built to last.

Every project begins with the coating manufacturer's published specifications. Surface preparation, environmental conditions, and application procedures are followed exactly as specified to maximize adhesion, durability, and long-term performance.

That standard was established through a union apprenticeship where proper preparation, product knowledge, and application techniques were considered the foundation of quality workmanship. Over the decades, those principles have carried into commercial, industrial, institutional, and high-end residential projects throughout the country.

From manufacturing facilities and distribution centers to churches, historic structures, executive offices, and custom residential finishes, every project is approached with the same disciplined standard.
